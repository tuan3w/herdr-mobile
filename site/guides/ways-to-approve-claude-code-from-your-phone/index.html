Ways to approve Claude Code from your phone

There are five common ways. The right one depends on whether you want a push alert, a terminal, or a list of every agent you run. I make herdr mobile, so read this knowing that.

Remote Control from Anthropic

Good when you only use Claude Code, you have a paid Claude plan, and you want the official route with an iPhone or Android app. Falls short when you want other agents, no account, or one list across several computers. It works through Anthropic's service, so your computer needs to reach it.

Hook notifications with ntfy

Small projects such as claude-remote-approver and claude-push turn a Claude Code permission request into a push notification with Allow and Deny. Good when you want the least moving parts and are happy with a notification and two buttons. Falls short when you want to see the whole session, other agents, or files. Notifications go through an ntfy server, so self-host it if that matters to you. Check each project for what it does when you do not answer in time.

An SSH terminal on your phone

Termius, or Termux with Tailscale and tmux, gives you a full terminal. Good when you want complete control and already live in the terminal. Falls short when you want an approval screen: you type everything on a phone keyboard, and nothing tells you an agent is waiting.

Hosted mobile apps for coding agents

Several apps, including Happy Coder and Paseo, add a mobile client for Claude Code and other agents, often with a wrapper command and QR pairing. Good when you want push, voice or a polished client and accept their pairing and relay model. Check for each one: does a relay or account sit in the middle, what is its licence, and which phones it supports. Their docs answer this better than I can.

herdr mobile

An Android app that reads agents from herdr over direct SSH. Good when you run several agents on several computers, want one list of who is waiting, want to read the exact command before approving, and want no relay and no account. A risky answer, such as a push or an always allow, needs a hold, and nothing is approved for you or on a timeout.

Falls short when you use an iPhone or a Windows computer, want push by default (notifications are off by default and cost battery), or need Claude Code approvals verified on a phone. Today they are checked on a computer, not a phone. It needs herdr on the computer.

Which one

  • Claude Code only, official, paid plan: Remote Control.
  • Just an alert with two buttons: a hook with ntfy.
  • You like the terminal: an SSH app.
  • Several agents on several computers, on Android, no account: herdr mobile.
